About Best AI Datasets

Best AI Datasets (bestaidatasets.com) is an independent research site. It has one job: to give a clear, current and honest answer to the question "what are the best AI datasets?"

Who runs this site

The site is run as an independent editorial project within a small network of single-topic guides. It is not affiliated with any dataset publisher, hub or cloud provider on our list. We don't invent authors or credentials, so pages carry the site's name rather than made-up bylines.

What we do and don't claim

  • We compile and compare information that dataset publishers make public on their own sites, and we link to those official sources.
  • We do not claim to have tested, trained on or audited any dataset or product.
  • We do not publish fake reviews, ratings or testimonials.
  • Inclusion and ranking are not for sale.

How to use this site

Treat our list as a well-sourced starting point. Before using any dataset, check its license, where its data came from and how recent it is on the official page. Read our methodology to see how we rank, or go straight to the ranked list.

Corrections

If a fact on this site is out of date, the official page linked from each entry is the authoritative source. We fix errors when we re-check the list.
