How we researched and ranked the best AI datasets

Last updated: 2026-11-09

What we compared

The phrase "AI datasets" covers two things: places to find datasets (hubs, repositories, search engines and cloud catalogs) and specific large datasets that are widely used to train AI models. We include both because people searching this phrase want both. Every pick is an organization or dataset that exists and has an official page.

How we researched

  • We read each source's own official pages and documentation in November 2026, including dataset cards, storage and pricing docs, and about pages. Each entry links to them.
  • We used general web research only to build the list of candidates, then confirmed each claim on the official page.
  • We only report numbers the source publishes itself, such as UCI's 690 datasets, Data.gov's 532,480 datasets and Dolma's 3 trillion tokens. Where a figure is dated, we say so; for example, Kaggle's 500,000+ datasets figure is its own count as of February 2025.
  • If we couldn't read a page directly (OpenML's homepage blocked our automated reader), we say so and rely on that organization's own documentation.

How we ranked

We weighed each source on:

  1. Usefulness for AI and ML work: how many of the common tasks (text, vision, multimodal, tabular, pretraining) it serves well.
  2. Documentation and versioning: dataset cards, datasheets, versions and removal processes.
  3. Licensing clarity: whether licenses are stated and can be filtered.
  4. Access and cost: free access, programmatic download, cloud options.
  5. Freshness and scale: how recently it was updated and how large it is.

General hubs rank higher because they serve the most needs. Specialist datasets and repositories rank by how well they serve their own niche. The ranking is our editorial judgment from published information.

What we did not do

We did not train models on these datasets, audit their contents, or test the products hands-on. We do not publish user reviews. No source paid to be included or to get a better position.

Updates

We re-check facts and links when we update the page. The "Last updated" date shows the most recent check.
